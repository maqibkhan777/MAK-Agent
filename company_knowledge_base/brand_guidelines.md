# Enterprise Brand Guidelines & Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

## 🎯 Corporate Identity & Tone of Voice
- **Brand Voice**: Professional, authoritative, confident, yet accessible and customer-centric.
- **Tone**: Analytical for financial strategy; energetic, persuasive, and clear for marketing copy.
- **Core Value Proposition**: "Empowering enterprise decision-making with autonomous AI precision and institutional-grade rigor."

## 💰 Pricing & Product Tiers
1. **Starter Tier**: $499/month — Essential financial modeling & basic SEO analytics.
2. **Professional Tier**: $1,999/month — Full multi-agent department execution, custom WACC/NPV models, and dedicated account manager.
3. **Enterprise Custom Tier**: $4,999+/month — Unlimited multi-department workflows, custom RAG integrations, and 24/7 dedicated support.

## 📢 Marketing & Copywriting Guidelines
- **Headline Standard**: Always lead with clear quantitative benefits (e.g., "Scale ROI by 40% with Autonomous Intelligence").
- **Calls to Action (CTA)**: Direct response CTAs only (e.g., "Schedule Your Executive Demo Today").
- **Prohibited Phrases**: Never use generic fluff like "cutting-edge solution" or "synergistic paradigm shift" without backing quantitative metrics.

## 🏛️ Finance & Audit Compliance Rules
- **Minimum Hurdle Rate**: Standard enterprise hurdle rate is 8.5% WACC.
- **Audit Requirement**: All capital projects above $10M require Financial Controller GAAP/IFRS review.
